# Variant A: generation first (PDF pages 13-23; frames 9-13)
1. Writing one record, one item at a time (pages 13-19, 7 clicks): source PUF, year 2015, filer and AGI group set first; then married, dependents, age range with probability bars; filing status and credit children; wages over 84 bands (zero + 73 positive percentile bands); then every other tax item in random order, totals added up from parts.
2. The rest of the record (page 20): table of the survey phases in pipeline order (CPS ages, SCF balance sheet at the record's wave, CEX consumption, CPS person items, SIPP tips), what each draws and sees; wave shares 55/33/12; consistency rules; dependent filers get no survey items.
3. What one step returns, and how it becomes dollars (page 21): 84 bands (zero, 10 loss, 73 positive at 2/1/0.1/0.01-point percentile steps); a band becomes a real value from that band, then grown to 2019.
4. Where the probabilities come from: training (page 22): one SCF record with hidden items, before/after bars; hide one-to-all items, maximum likelihood, 1.4 million parameters, 80 passes, top records count extra.
5. Post-training (page 23): 400,000 filers by AGI group plus CPS non-filers; starting weight = group's 2015 returns / records drawn; smallest KL reweighting so each of 753 targets is within 1 SE (SCF 0.5).
