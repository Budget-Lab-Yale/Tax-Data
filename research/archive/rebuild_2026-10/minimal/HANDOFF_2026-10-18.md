## 7. 19 October

The full write-ups are `34_RESULTS.md` sections 3–5 (pretraining) and
`36_RESULTS.md` (business targets in the tilt). The short version follows.

**Why the generated files hold too few rare business holders.**

1. **Dropout (0.1) is the main cause.**
   - Training matches the data's shares on average with the dropout noise
     present. For rare outcomes, removing the noise at generation lowers
     the chance: the mean-versus-median gap of a log model.
   - Two fixes, which test about the same: train with
     `MINIMAL_DROPOUT=0`, or generate with dropout on
     (`MINIMAL_GEN_DROPOUT=1`, no retraining). Each closes about half the
     gap. Partnership nonpassive holders go from 1.18% to 1.22–1.27%,
     against the PUF's 1.33%.
   - Which to adopt is John's call, not yet made.
2. **Three training changes did not help, and can be dropped:** longer
   training (160 passes), the loss weighted by slots hidden, and
   oversampling rare business holders in training.
3. **The rest, about 5–8% of rare holders, comes from the model's answers
   not fitting one joint distribution.** A file drawn in one order holds
   fewer holders than the same model, reading it in another order, expects.
   A resampling pass after generation (Gibbs) was tried and failed: the
   file drifts with every sweep (`34_resample.py`).
4. **Mean business income stays low in every version:** 6–15% short.
   Without dropout the three seeds give 0.93, 0.92 and 0.85.

**Business targets in the 2019 tilt (new, `36_*`).**
- SOI Table 1.4 publishes net income and net loss, returns and amount, by
  AGI, for six families. Counting each return's net per family matches SOI
  2015 within 0–5%.
- On the two worst stacks (Ns1, HL1), the targets are hit for 1–2 points of
  effective sample at 6 AGI groups. 15 brackets is too fine.
- A target built from the 2015 PUF is good to about 5% for large income
  items and off 15–50% for losses and small families.
- **The rule is not decided.** John: "sort this out based on vibes and
  judgment later". The open questions are listed in `36_RESULTS.md`.

**Left to the model, which the tilt cannot reach:**
- too many units hold both partnership and S corporation items, 1.14–1.33
  of the PUF's share in every model;
- nonpassive losses are low;
- the passive share is somewhat high.
